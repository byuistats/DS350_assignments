Good data science hinges on good questions. Watch the videos here to help you ask questions, especially as you develop questions for your semester project. NOTE: These LinkedInLearning videos require BYUI authentication. Follow these steps to play the videos: Free LinkedInLearning for College Students (Step by Step).

  • Harness the Power of Questions
  • Run Question Meetings
  • Clarify Key Terms

  1. Read the semester project assignment *****LINK*****

  2. Develop a few novel questions (at least 4) that data can answer. Consider this a brainstorm of what you might address for your semester project.

    • Look for other examples of people addressing your question, or a similar question, online. Include a url to their work if possible.
    • Present your question to a data scientist or data analyst (this can include teachers) to get feedback on the quality of the question and if it can be addressed in two months.

  3. Edit the readme.md file in the folder for this assignment so that it contains your potential questions and feedback. Push it to GitHub to be easily shared.

  1. Get feedback from four to six people on their interest in your questions and summarize this feedback in the readme.md file you edited as part of the pre class tasks.
